# Delete your GoldenPaw account or data

<div class="card">
<strong>Fastest:</strong> in the app, open <strong>Settings → Delete my account</strong>. It takes effect immediately.
</div>

## What each option removes

| Option | Where | What is deleted |
|---|---|---|
| **Delete my account** | Settings (signed in) | Your sign-in and email address; every care team you own, with all its shared pets and records; your membership in other teams; your registered devices and push tokens; your subscription status record. |
| **Delete all data** | Settings | Everything stored on this device: pets, medicines, logs, photos, scanned documents, reminders and settings. |
| **Uninstall the app** | Your phone | Everything stored on that device. |

Deleting your account does **not** cancel a Plus subscription. Cancel it in your [Google Play subscriptions](https://play.google.com/store/account/subscriptions) or App Store account settings first.

Records you added to **someone else's** care team stay in that team (they're part of that pet's care history), but your name and account are unlinked from it. Ask the team owner if you want those entries removed.

## Can't open the app?

Email [{{ site.contact_email }}](mailto:{{ site.contact_email }}?subject=GoldenPaw%20account%20deletion) **from the email address you signed in with**, with the subject "Account deletion". We'll confirm it's you and delete the account and its data within 30 days, and reply when it's done.

Support messages you sent are deleted within 24 months, or sooner on request.
