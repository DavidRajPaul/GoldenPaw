# Calm, caring support for your senior pet

<p class="lede">GoldenPaw keeps medicines, daily check-ins, symptoms and vet visits for an older dog or cat in one gentle place, and shares them with the people who help care for them.</p>

- **Medicine reminders** that work offline, with a double-dose guard for shared care.
- **Daily check-ins** and good-day tracking to see how your pet is really doing.
- **Vet-ready reports** of symptoms, weights and visits.
- **Care team** for family and sitters, with access that ends when you choose.
- **Private by design**: records stay on your phone unless you choose to share them.
