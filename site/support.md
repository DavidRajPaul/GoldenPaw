## Common questions

### My reminders didn't go off
Open **Settings → Reminders health check**. It shows anything blocking notifications (permission, battery restrictions, exact alarms) and takes you to the right setting. Reminders are scheduled on your phone, so they work without internet.

### How do I cancel Plus or get a refund?
- **Google Play:** Play Store → profile → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions. Within about 48 hours of a charge you can request a refund from Google directly; after that, email us.
- **App Store:** Settings → your name → Subscriptions. Refunds are handled by Apple at [reportaproblem.apple.com](https://reportaproblem.apple.com).

You keep Plus until the end of the period you paid for.

### I paid but Plus isn't showing
Make sure you're signed in to the same Google or Apple account you bought with, then use **Restore purchases** on the Plus screen. Still missing? Message us with your Support ID.

### Is an offer notification real?
Offers only open inside GoldenPaw and are paid through Google Play or the App Store. We never ask for card details by message, email or on a website. Offer notifications are off unless you turn them on in Settings.

### How does the care team work?
Turn on sharing in **Care team**, then invite family or a sitter with a code. Everyone sees the same medicines, doses and check-ins, and a sitter's access ends on the date you choose.
